Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how lang ("we", "the app") collects, uses and protects your information when you sign in with your Google account.

1. Information we collect

When you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture

We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and identify your account in the app
  • To let you sign in securely
  • To contact you about your account if needed

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

lang's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data storage and security

Your information is stored securely and only for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access.

6. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time by emailing kslang.xd@gmail.com. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact
